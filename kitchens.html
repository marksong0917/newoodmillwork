THE CRAFT

Built to the room, not to a catalogue.

Every Newood kitchen starts with a site measure: walls and corners checked as they actually are, cabinetry drawn to the room, then cut, edged and assembled in our Concord shop.

The team that builds your kitchen installs it — drawers aligned by hand, doors hung and adjusted on site, panels scribed to walls that are rarely straight. Nothing is handed off.

  • Blum hinges and lift systems
