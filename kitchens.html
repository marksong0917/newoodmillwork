QUESTIONS

Kitchens, answered

How long does a custom kitchen renovation take?

Most kitchen cabinetry projects take four to eight weeks from final measurements to installation, depending on the size of the kitchen and our current shop schedule. We'll give you a firm timeline once your design is locked in.

Do you work with my designer or contractor?

Yes. We regularly work alongside homeowners, interior designers, and general contractors, and can join an existing renovation team or manage the cabinetry portion directly with you.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Concord, Ontario and install kitchens throughout the Greater Toronto Area, including Vaughan, Toronto, Richmond Hill, Markham, and Mississauga.
