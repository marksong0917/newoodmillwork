QUESTIONS

Quick answers

How much will my kitchen cost?

Every project is quoted individually. For a fast ballpark, try our free online kitchen estimator — it takes under a minute and needs no contact info. For an exact price, we measure your space and quote in writing.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on scope and are confirmed in writing before we start. Most kitchens are built and installed within weeks of design approval — your quote includes the schedule.

Do you design, build, and install?

Yes — one team handles measurement, design drawings, fabrication in our Concord shop, and installation. No handoffs.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Concord and serve Vaughan and the Greater Toronto Area, including Richmond Hill, Markham, Aurora, Newmarket, and Toronto. Commercial projects run across Ontario.
