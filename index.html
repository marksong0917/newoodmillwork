ABOUT

About Newood Millwork

Newood Millwork Inc. is a custom millwork shop in Concord, Ontario, serving the GTA and surrounding areas. QUESTIONS

Quick answers

What does the design and site measure include?

Every project is quoted individually. Once you accept an estimate, the in-person design & site measure is $399 + HST for a kitchen and $200 + HST for a closet — paid by an emailed payment link, non-refundable, and credited toward your order when you order within 90 days of paying the fee (closets up to $400; kitchens up to $599). Online booking covers the GTA; outside the GTA, bookings are arranged manually.

How many drawing revisions are included?

The design & site measure includes three rounds of drawing revisions; further rounds are $200 + HST per additional three rounds.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on scope and are confirmed in writing before we start. Most kitchens are built and installed within weeks of design approval — your quote includes the schedule.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Concord and serve Vaughan and the Greater Toronto Area, including Richmond Hill, Markham, Aurora, Newmarket, and Toronto. Commercial projects run across Ontario.

What materials and hardware do you use?

Full-extension soft-close drawers, Blum hinges and lift systems, and panels and finishes chosen for your project — all confirmed in your written quote.
