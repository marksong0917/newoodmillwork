ABOUT

Custom cabinetry, built around your space.

NEWOOD MILLWORK INC is a Canadian-owned and operated company that designs and builds custom cabinetry, closets, and commercial millwork out of our shop in Concord, Ontario. Every piece is measured, cut, and installed to fit the space it's built for. We work with homeowners, contractors, and commercial clients on projects of any size, from a single closet to a full storefront fit-out.
