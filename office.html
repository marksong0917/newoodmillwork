QUESTIONS

Office cabinetry, answered

Do you build both home offices and commercial office spaces?

Yes. We build home office cabinetry and built-in desks for residential clients, as well as reception counters and workstation millwork for commercial office spaces.

Can cabinetry include integrated lighting and cable management?

Yes. We regularly build in LED lighting for display cabinets and shelving, along with cable pass-throughs and outlets for desks and workstations.

What's the typical timeline for an office cabinetry project?

Most home office builds take three to six weeks from final measurements to installation. Commercial office and reception millwork timelines depend on scope, and we'll confirm a schedule once the design is set.
