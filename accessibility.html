Our Commitment

Newood Millwork Inc. is committed to treating all people in a way that allows them to maintain their dignity and independence. We aim to make our website, our showroom communication, and our services accessible to people of all abilities, consistent with the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA).

What We Do

We build and maintain this website with accessibility in mind — semantic page structure, text alternatives for images, keyboard-friendly navigation, and readable colour contrast — and we use WCAG 2.1 guidance when updating pages. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and some pages may still be improving.

Feedback & Alternate Formats

If you have difficulty accessing any part of this website or our materials, please contact us. On request, we will provide information in an alternate format that works for you, and we welcome feedback about accessibility at any time.
