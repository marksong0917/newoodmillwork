QUESTIONS

Vanities, answered

Can you build a vanity for double sinks?

Yes. We regularly build single and double-sink vanities, sized and configured around the plumbing rough-in and counter you've chosen.

Do you work with the countertop and plumbing installers?

Yes. We coordinate cabinet dimensions and sink cutouts with your countertop fabricator and plumber so everything lines up on install day.

What finishes are available for a bathroom vanity?

We build in a range of finishes, from painted shaker fronts to wood-tone veneers, with your choice of hardware.
