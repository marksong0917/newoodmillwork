QUESTIONS

Commercial projects, answered

Do you work with general contractors and property managers on commercial builds?

Yes. We regularly work as a subcontractor on commercial fit-outs, coordinating directly with general contractors, property managers, and designers on schedule and scope.

Can you match finishes across multiple store locations?

Yes. For multi-location retail and restaurant clients, we build to a consistent spec and finish so each location matches, whether it's one store or a rollout across several.

What's the typical timeline for a commercial fit-out?

Timelines depend heavily on scope, but most retail and café fixture packages run six to twelve weeks from final drawings to installation. We'll give you a firm schedule once the scope is set.
