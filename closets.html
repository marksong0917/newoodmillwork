THE CRAFT

Storage planned around what you own.

A closet works when everything has a considered place. We start with what you hang, fold, and store — long dresses, suits, shoes, bags — and plan each section around the wardrobe, not the other way around.

Walk-ins, reach-ins, and wardrobes are built in our Concord shop from the same certified panels as our kitchens, with soft-close drawers and hardware chosen to be used every day, for years.

Closets are also the entire focus of our sister brand, Havenwest Closets & Storage Solutions.

  • Long-hang, double-hang, and shelving planned per wardrobe
  • Soft-close drawer banks and pull-out racks
  • Shoe storage and accessory drawers
  • LED lighting options
  • Built-in wardrobes and wall units
  • Mudroom and laundry storage
