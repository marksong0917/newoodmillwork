QUESTIONS

Closets, answered

How do you measure for a custom closet?

We come to your home to measure the space directly, so every shelf, rod, and drawer is built to fit the exact room, not a standard size.

What finishes and materials are available?

We build in a range of finishes and hardware, including glass display doors, integrated lighting, and soft-close drawers, and can match existing millwork in your home.

How long does a closet installation take?

A single closet or wardrobe typically installs in one to two days once the cabinetry is built. Larger walk-in systems may take a few days longer.
