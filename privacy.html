PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

The Short Version

We collect only what you give us through our contact form or by reaching out directly — your name, email, phone number, and whatever project details you choose to share. We use it for exactly one purpose: to respond to you about your project. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone, ever.

What We Collect

  • Information you submit through our contact form: name, email, phone number, project type, and any details or photos you send us
  • Information you send directly by phone, email, or in person
  • Basic, non-identifying website usage data (such as which pages are visited) if analytics tools are in use, for the purpose of understanding how our site is used

How We Use It

We use your information solely to respond to your inquiry, prepare a quote, schedule a consultation, or complete a project you've hired us for. Our contact form is built to open a pre-filled email in your own email application — your information is sent directly to us and is not stored on a server or database.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal information to any third party
  • We do not trade or rent your information for marketing purposes
  • We do not share your information with anyone outside of completing your project, except where required by law

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use standard, privacy-respecting analytics tools to understand general traffic patterns (for example, which pages are popular). This data is aggregated and not used to identify you personally.

Your Rights

You can ask us at any time what information we have about you, and request that we delete it. Just contact us using the information below.

Questions?

If you have any questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us directly.
